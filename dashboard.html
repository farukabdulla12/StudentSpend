            About StudentSpend

            StudentSpend is a simple and student-friendly personal finance management platform designed to help students track their income, expenses, budgets, and spending habits in one place.

            Whether you receive money from home, spend on food, transportation, education, or entertainment, StudentSpend helps you understand where your money goes and manage it more effectively.

            Our goal is to make personal finance simple, organized, and accessible for every student.
